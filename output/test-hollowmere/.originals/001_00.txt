Chapter 2: Cold Tea

Captain Voss never broke the ice with words; he broke it with kettles.

By the time Mira reached the kitchen, the tea was already cold, and so, it seemed, was the welcome.

“Sit,” he said. Mira sat.

Somewhere above them, the great lamp turned once and sighed like an old dog.

“The ghost says you’re late,” Mira offered. “He says that a lot,” Voss muttered into his cup.
